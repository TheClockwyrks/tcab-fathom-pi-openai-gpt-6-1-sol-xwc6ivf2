# Fathom

Below the reach of sunlight, your glow is both a guide and a giveaway. Thread a flooded maze as a tiny luminous forager, gathering plankton one cold spark at a time. Every meal brightens the water around you. Every brighter passage makes you easier to find.

The trench remembers where you have been, but you can only see that memory inside the circle of light you carry. Send a sonar crest curling around corners to chart what lies ahead. Watch a distant amber glimmer drift closer. Is it a harmless drifter worth a bonus, or a Lanternjaw hiding its teeth beneath the same golden bell?

Three hunters listen to different secrets. The Lanternjaw follows your light. The Gloamfin hears through rock, and your own sonar can lead it straight to you. The Flarefish burns a sudden window into the abyss, revealing both the maze and the danger inside it. Ink can break a light hunter’s pursuit, but it will never silence your footsteps.

Graze the last mote to clear the maze and descend. The depths bring more hunters and shorter sonar reach, while the passages you thought you knew slip back beyond your window. Keep moving, round the next corner, and decide how much light you dare to carry.
